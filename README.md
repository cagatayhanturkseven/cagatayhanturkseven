## English

Faculty member at Mersin University Faculty of Medicine, Department of Biophysics. My work centres on the recording and analysis of biological signals, experimental models at the cell and tissue level, clinical data analysis, and research software that supports this work.

### Research areas

- Electrobiophysics: recording and analysis of electrical activity at the cell and tissue level
- Cardiac biosignals: digital processing of ECG and haemodynamic measurements
- Neuroscience: biophysical basis of nervous system function
- Experimental regeneration models
- Clinical data analysis and biostatistics
- Assessment tools for medical education
